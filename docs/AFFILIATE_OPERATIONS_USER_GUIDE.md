# Affiliate Operations — User Guide

This is the complete guide to GWS Business Suite's Affiliate Operations cluster: **CJ Ads**
(`/admin/cj-ads`), **Affiliate Suggestions** (`/admin/affiliate-suggestions`), and **Affiliate
Analytics** (`/admin/affiliate-analytics`). Together these three pages connect this site to
Commission Junction (CJ.com), decide which affiliate offers go into which blog articles, and
report on what happened after they did.

This guide is text-only (no screenshots) — see the note at the end of
[`docs/USER_GUIDES.md`](USER_GUIDES.md) for why, and how that may change later.

## Core concepts

- **Advertiser** (CJ calls these "advertisers" or "merchants"): a brand you have a relationship
  with in your CJ account — e.g. a retailer or SaaS company running a CJ affiliate program. CJ
  Ads Manager only ever shows advertisers whose relationship status is Joined, Active, or
  Approved ("My Advertisers") — advertisers you haven't joined are never listed here.
- **Offer** (a "link" in CJ's own terms, "active ad" in this page's own labels): one specific
  trackable CJ link belonging to an advertiser — a product link, a banner, a deep link — with its
  own name, category, tracking URL, optional image, and an optional promotion end date. Every
  advertiser can have any number of offers.
- **Sync**: pulling live data from CJ's API into this app's own `AffiliateOffer` table.
  Advertiser sync ("My Advertisers") and link sync (an advertiser's individual offers) are
  separate operations — connecting your account only pulls the advertiser roster; you (or the
  hourly scheduled sync) still need to sync links before there's anything to place in an article.
- **Placement**: a real, persisted assignment of one offer to one article, with a unique
  `{{CJ_AD_xxxxxxxx}}` token inserted into that article's markdown body. At render time the token
  is swapped for an HTML "Sponsored Pick" card. A placement can be created two ways — manually in
  the Article Editor, or by applying a Suggestion — see
  [Two ways an offer actually lands in an article](#two-ways-an-offer-actually-lands-in-an-article).
- **Rotation** (the 48-hour blog ad rotation): a separate, non-editorial mechanism that assigns
  one offer per active published article automatically, re-picked every 48 hours, and appended to
  the rendered article at serve time rather than inserted into the article's own markdown. A
  rotation assignment is not a placement — it doesn't touch the article's saved content or
  revision history at all.
- **Suggestion**: an AI-proposed offer-to-article match, generated by SentinelGPT (via this app's
  self-hosted Ollama) reading the article's title, keywords, and body against your synced offer
  catalog. A suggestion is inert data — nothing changes on the live site until an admin clicks
  Apply.
- **Click / commission tracking**: every reader click on a placement's or rotation's rendered ad
  card routes through this app's own `/go/{placementId}` redirect, which logs a click row and
  then forwards the reader to the real CJ tracking URL. Commission (sale/payout) data is a
  separate, best-effort import from CJ's own commissions API — see
  [Syncing commissions](#syncing-commissions).

## Connecting your CJ account

Open **CJ Ads Manager** (`/admin/cj-ads`) and click **Connect CJ Account**. The modal asks for:

- **Developer Key** — your CJ Personal Access Token. Stored encrypted (via this app's Data
  Protection secret protector); once saved, it's never shown again in plain text, and the modal
  offers a show/hide eye toggle only while you're actively typing it in.
- **Publisher ID (CID)** — your CJ publisher account id.
- **Website ID** (required before links can sync) — the numeric Website ID CJ assigned to this
  specific site. CJ needs this to generate site-specific tracking links; it is not your site's
  URL or your Publisher ID.
- **Endpoint URL** — defaults to `https://commissions.api.cj.com/query`; only change this if CJ
  tells you to.
- **Max Results** — how many records to request per call (1–500, default 100).

Three buttons act on this form:

- **Test Connection** validates the credentials against CJ without saving or importing anything,
  and reports a preview partner count on success.
- **Save Credentials** stores the form's values (including the automatic-rotation toggle's
  current state) without calling CJ at all.
- **Connect And Pull Partners** validates, saves, and immediately pulls your My Advertisers
  roster from CJ in one action — this is the normal first-time flow.

If a previously-saved Developer Key can no longer be decrypted (for example, the server's
encryption key ring changed since it was saved), the page detects this on load, clears the
unreadable value, opens the connector modal automatically, and asks you to re-enter your token.

The **Connected** / **Not Connected** badge at the top reflects whether a Developer Key and
Publisher ID are currently saved — it does not re-validate against CJ on every page load.

## Browsing advertisers and syncing links

Once connected, **My Advertisers (Joined/Active)** lists every advertiser CJ returned with a
Joined/Active/Approved relationship, with a live search box (matches advertiser name or id) and a
**Clear Search** button. Each row shows a relationship badge, an active-ad count, and a category
badge if CJ supplied one.

Click an advertiser's row to expand it and load its individual offers on demand (only fetched the
first time you expand a given advertiser in a session). Each offer row shows its name, category,
promotion end date (or "-" if it never expires), and a **Choose Ad** button — this opens the
offer's raw CJ tracking URL directly in a new tab so you can preview or copy it. It does **not**
by itself insert anything into an article; that's a separate step (see
[Two ways an offer actually lands in an article](#two-ways-an-offer-actually-lands-in-an-article)).

Two sync actions live in the Connector card:

- **Sync Links for [advertiser]** (inside an expanded advertiser panel) — calls CJ's live Link
  Search API for that one advertiser and fully replaces its catalog of offers with what CJ
  returns. If the fetch parses to zero links (a schema hiccup, a program with no active links
  right now, etc.), the existing catalog for that advertiser is deliberately left untouched
  rather than wiped, and the status message says so.
- **Sync All Links** (top of the page) — loops every advertiser in your roster and syncs each
  one's links in turn, with a small delay between calls as a courtesy to CJ's API. One
  advertiser's failure doesn't abort the run; failures are collected and listed under "Some
  advertisers failed to sync." This button and the AffiliateAnalytics page's commission sync
  share one process-wide lock with the hourly scheduled sync (see
  [The scheduled background sync](#the-scheduled-background-sync)) — if a scheduled run is
  already in progress, clicking either manual sync just tells you to try again in a moment
  instead of running concurrently.

**Refresh My Advertisers** re-pulls the roster itself (not links) from CJ — useful after you join
a new program in CJ's own dashboard.

## Manually importing offers

CJ's Sync/commissions API only returns advertiser relationships, not clickable tracking links —
**Import Manually** (per advertiser) exists for advertisers whose links you'd rather export from
CJ's own "Get Links" / deep-link tool and paste or upload here, instead of (or in addition to)
live link sync.

- **Format**: Auto-detect, CSV, TSV, or JSON. Auto-detect looks for a leading `[`/`{` (JSON), a
  tab character (TSV), or falls back to CSV.
- **Replace this advertiser's existing offers**: checked by default — a full replace rather than
  a merge.
- Paste text directly, or upload a file (`.csv`, `.tsv`, `.json`, `.txt`, capped at 5 MB).
- Each row needs at minimum an offer name and a tracking URL; rows missing either are silently
  skipped. Delimited rows can include a header row (recognized by column names like `offer`,
  `category`, `tracking`, `promotionend`) or rely on positional columns
  (`offerName, category, trackingUrl, promotionEndsAt`) if there's no header.

## The 48-hour blog ad rotation

Below the Connector card, the **48-hour blog ad rotation** panel shows a stable card widget: one
CJ offer per currently-active published post, automatically assigned and held for 48 hours before
being re-picked. It shows:

- **Posts assigned / active posts** — how many published posts currently have a live rotation
  assignment out of the total eligible.
- **Eligible CJ links** — how many synced offers currently qualify (must belong to a
  Joined/Active/Approved advertiser, have a non-empty tracking URL, and not be past any promotion
  end date). If this is zero, no posts will get an ad until you sync links successfully.
- **Next rotation** — the soonest expiry time among current assignments, or "Not scheduled" if
  nothing is currently assigned.

Selection logic: for each eligible post needing (re-)assignment, the offer with the fewest
existing rotation assignments is picked first (spreading exposure evenly across your catalog),
tie-broken alphabetically; the previous advertiser assigned to that same post is avoided when a
different advertiser is available, so the same post doesn't show the same brand two rotations in
a row.

- **Pause rotation / Resume rotation** toggles `AutomaticArticleRotationEnabled`. Pausing leaves
  all existing article content and assignment history untouched — it just stops picking new ones.
  Resuming immediately triggers a refresh.
- **Rotate now** forces an immediate re-pick for every active post (ending all current
  assignments and picking fresh ones), regardless of whether their 48-hour window has actually
  elapsed.

A rotation assignment is rendered by appending a "Sponsored Pick" card to the end of the article's
HTML at serve time — it is never written into the article's own markdown, so it never shows up in
the Article Editor's placement list and never affects the article's revision history.

## Two ways an offer actually lands in an article

This is worth understanding because none of it happens automatically from the CJ Ads page itself:

1. **Manual placement** (Article Editor → Placements panel, not covered page-by-page here): pick
   an advertiser and one of its synced offers, optionally set custom call-to-action text, and
   save. This creates the placement row but does **not** insert its token into the article body
   for you — a status message reminds you to use "Insert at Cursor" to actually drop the
   `{{CJ_AD_xxxxxxxx}}` token where you want the card to appear.
2. **Applying a SentinelGPT suggestion** (Affiliate Suggestions page — see next section):
   applying inserts the token automatically, spread across the article by paragraph position
   (roughly the 1/4, 1/2, and 3/4 marks for the first, second, and third placement respectively,
   or appended to the end for very short articles), so cards don't cluster at the bottom.

Both paths ultimately create the same kind of row (`ArticleAffiliatePlacement`) and render through
the same `/go/{placementId}` click-tracked link — the only difference is who decides the offer and
where the token lands.

## Reviewing and accepting a SentinelGPT suggestion

**Affiliate Suggestions** (`/admin/affiliate-suggestions`) is the review queue for AI-proposed
offer-to-article matches. Nothing here goes live until you click Apply.

**Generate for All Articles** re-runs matching across every non-trashed article. For each article,
up to 3 offers are chosen from up to the 40 most-recently-updated eligible offers in your catalog
(active promotions only), by asking a local Ollama model to read the article's title, primary and
secondary keywords, and up to the first 3,000 characters of its body against the numbered offer
list, and to pick only offers that are genuinely topically relevant — it can return fewer than 3,
or none, if nothing fits well. Running this replaces that article's existing *Pending* suggestions
only; anything already Applied or Dismissed for that article is left alone as history, not
regenerated. A bulk run can take a while on a large blog, and per-article failures are collected
and shown rather than aborting the whole run.

Suggestions are grouped by article. Each group shows the article's title, status, an **Open
Article** link (opens the editor in a new tab), an **Apply All** button, and a table of individual
suggestions with the advertiser, link name, category, and the model's one-sentence reasoning for
why it fits.

- **Apply** (per suggestion) turns that one suggestion into a real placement — creating the
  `ArticleAffiliatePlacement` row and inserting its slot token into the article body — and marks
  the suggestion Applied.
- **Apply All** does the same for every pending suggestion on that article in rank order, in one
  save.
- **Dismiss** marks a suggestion Dismissed without creating any placement. Dismissed and Applied
  suggestions never reappear in this list and are never silently regenerated by a later
  "Generate" run for that same article, whether you click it or the scheduled sync does.

New articles also get an automatic suggestion pass the moment they're published from the Article
Editor (best-effort — a failure here never blocks the publish itself), so in practice this page
usually already has something waiting after you publish, without needing to click Generate
manually.

## Reading the analytics dashboard

**Affiliate Analytics** (`/admin/affiliate-analytics`) reports on what already happened: reader
clicks (tracked directly by this app) and imported CJ commission data (best-effort, see below).
Every number on this page — including the "Total Commission (all-time)" tile — is actually scoped
to activity from the **last 90 days**, capped at the 5,000 most recent rows for clicks and for
commission records independently; despite the "all-time" label on that tile, older activity drops
out of every figure on this page once it ages past that window.

- **Total Clicks** / **Total Commission (all-time)** — headline tiles for the windowed data
  described above.
- **Clicks by Advertiser** — click counts and last-click timestamp, grouped by advertiser,
  highest first.
- **Clicks by Article** — click counts per article, with a link to the live article if it still
  has a slug.
- **Revenue by Advertiser (CJ commission import)** — transaction count, total sale amount, and
  total commission amount per advertiser, from imported CJ commission records, highest commission
  first.
- **Recent Clicks** — the 50 most recent individual click events, each showing advertiser,
  article title, and timestamp.

A click is only ever counted once per placement within a rolling 30-minute window — a
browser-back, refresh, or accidental double-click on the same ad doesn't inflate the count, though
the reader is still redirected normally every time. This dedupe does not key on IP address or any
other reader-identifying signal (deliberately, to avoid collecting PII for what's an internal
analytics feature) and isn't meant as fraud protection — it only smooths out the cheap, common
double-counting cases.

## Syncing commissions

**Sync Commissions Now** calls CJ's commissions GraphQL API and upserts results into this app's
own commission table, matched by CJ's own commission id so re-running never creates duplicates and
naturally picks up status changes (e.g. Pending → Closed) on the next sync. It shares the same
process-wide lock as the hourly scheduled sync and the manual link syncs on the CJ Ads page — if
one is already running, this one politely declines and asks you to try again shortly.

The page carries a standing warning banner about this feature specifically: the exact field names
CJ's commissions API uses for sale/commission amounts have **not been independently verified** in
this environment. A schema mismatch on CJ's end doesn't produce an error — it just imports zero
rows, silently, with the sync's own message reflecting that. Click counts are entirely unaffected
either way, since those are tracked directly by this app rather than imported from CJ.

## The scheduled background sync

An hourly background service (starting one minute after app boot, then every hour) runs the same
work as the manual buttons across all three pages, in sequence, so day-to-day operation doesn't
strictly require visiting any of these pages at all once your account is connected:

1. Syncs links for every advertiser in your roster (same as **Sync All Links**).
2. Generates suggestions, but **only** for articles that have zero suggestions and zero placements
   so far — this specifically avoids re-churning AI calls over articles you've already reviewed,
   while still catching newly published articles that somehow missed their publish-time pass.
3. Refreshes the 48-hour rotation (assigns any active post that doesn't currently have a live
   assignment; does not touch posts still within their 48-hour window unless empty).
4. Syncs commissions (same as **Sync Commissions Now**).

This service is a safe no-op if no CJ account is connected yet — it loops over whatever's in the
(empty) partner roster and moves on. Every step of a scheduled run is independently logged; a
failure in one step (e.g. commission sync) doesn't block the others.

## Known limitations

- **Commission field mapping is unverified.** CJ's commissions GraphQL schema hasn't been
  independently confirmed in this environment; a mismatch fails silently (zero rows imported, no
  error surfaced beyond the sync message) rather than throwing.
- **The "Total Commission (all-time)" label is misleading.** Every figure on the Analytics
  dashboard — clicks and commissions alike — is actually windowed to the last 90 days (and capped
  at 5,000 rows per table), not true all-time totals.
- **No dedicated tag/category filter on Analytics.** You can see clicks/revenue by advertiser or
  article, but there's no way to filter the dashboard itself by date range, advertiser, or
  article from the UI.
- **Click dedupe isn't fraud protection.** The 30-minute per-placement window only smooths out
  ordinary double-counting (refresh, back-button); it doesn't detect or block deliberate click
  fraud, and doesn't key on IP or any other reader signal by design.
- **"Choose Ad" on the CJ Ads page doesn't insert anything.** It only opens the raw CJ tracking
  URL in a new tab for preview/copy — creating an actual article placement always happens either
  in the Article Editor's Placements panel or by applying a Suggestion.
- **Manual placements require a manual "Insert at Cursor" step.** Saving a placement in the
  Article Editor does not automatically drop its token into the article body; only applying a
  Suggestion does that automatically.
- **Suggestions are matched by a local Ollama model, not CJ's own recommendation engine.** Match
  quality depends on the installed model and on how much useful text is in the article body/
  keywords; it can propose zero suggestions for an article if nothing in the catalog seems
  genuinely relevant, or be unavailable/timed out (30-second bound), in which case that article
  is simply skipped rather than erroring.
- **The 48-hour rotation is separate from editorial placements and can't be reviewed per-article
  before it goes live** — there's no approval step comparable to Suggestions; toggling it off is
  the only way to stop it before its next automatic pick.
- **Bulk operations (Sync All Links, Generate for All Articles) are sequential, not parallel**,
  so they can take a noticeable amount of time on a large advertiser roster or blog, and only one
  CJ-related sync (manual or scheduled) can run at a time app-wide.
